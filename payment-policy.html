Payment & Commitment Policy

Clear rules for optional paid accountability modes.

1. Optional paid accountability

RYZE includes a Regular mode with no commitment charge. Commitment and Daily Commitment are optional modes for users who deliberately want a financial accountability consequence.

2. Standard commitment charge

The current intended beta charge is RM2.50 per eligible occurrence when the user snoozes or misses a Commitment or Daily Commitment alarm.

3. One-charge rule

RYZE is designed to create no more than one financial commitment charge per alarm occurrence. A snooze followed by a miss on the same occurrence should still result in no more than the single RM2.50 charge.

4. Successful wake

When the applicable wake flow is successfully completed on time, no commitment charge is created for that occurrence.

5. Payment timing

During beta, commitment events may first appear as pending and may be settled later through the configured payment provider. A pending event is not treated as successfully paid until provider confirmation.

6. Payment status

RYZE may display statuses such as pending, processing, paid, failed or refunded.

7. No wallet or withdrawable balance

RYZE does not provide a stored-value wallet. “Money Kept” is a motivational estimate of charges avoided and cannot be withdrawn.

8. Consistency recovery programs

RYZE may test programs that recognize strong Daily Commitment consistency. If enabled, eligibility rules will be shown before the program applies. A preview or calculation does not by itself mean an automated refund has been executed.

9. Failed or disputed payments

If a payment fails, the related charge may remain unpaid or failed. Contact RYZE if you believe a duplicate, incorrect or unauthorized transaction occurred.

10. Card handling

RYZE is designed to use a third-party payment processor and does not intend to store full card numbers or card security codes.

11. Refunds and corrections

Confirmed duplicate or incorrect charges may be investigated and, where appropriate, corrected or refunded through the payment provider.
