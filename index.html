How it works

A better alarm is a decision system.

RYZE is designed around one idea: waking up should be intentional, not something you renegotiate while half-asleep.

01 / SET

Choose your alarm

Pick Regular, Commitment, or Daily Commitment.

02 / WAKE

Answer the alarm

Complete the wake interaction instead of instantly dismissing it.

03 / PROVE

Finish the challenge

A short maths challenge adds friction between tapping and actually waking.

04 / BUILD

Track consistency

Your Daily Commitment results build a record of successful and missed days.

Three alarm modes

Regular

  • No financial commitment
  • No Daily Commitment consistency impact

Commitment

RM2.50 / applicable snooze or miss
  • Optional financial consequence

Your official wake-up commitment for the day.

Payment transparency

RYZE doesn't want your RM2.50. We want you to keep it.

Paid commitment modes are optional. The charge exists as an accountability consequence, not as gambling, an investment, a wallet balance, or a chance-based reward.

✓
One charge maximum

A snooze followed by a miss on the same occurrence is not two separate charges.

✓
Wake on time = no charge

Complete the wake flow successfully and the RM2.50 consequence does not apply.

✓
No stored card numbers

Card details are handled by a third-party payment processor; RYZE is not designed to store full card credentials.
