Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Private beta

This policy reflects the current RYZE beta model and should be reviewed before a full commercial launch.

1. What RYZE is

RYZE is a wake-up accountability application that lets users create alarms, complete wake challenges, view consistency information and, where enabled, choose commitment-based alarms.

2. Information we may process

  • basic account or device identifiers;
  • alarm configuration and schedules;
  • wake-event results such as on-time completion, snooze or miss;
  • consistency and streak records;
  • payment-method connection status, provider references, charge status, settlement status and refund status;
  • technical diagnostics needed to operate and secure the service.

3. Payment information

RYZE is designed so full card details are entered with and processed by a third-party payment provider. RYZE may receive payment tokens, provider identifiers and status information but is not intended to store your full card number or card security code.

4. Why we use information

  • to operate alarms and wake challenges;
  • to calculate Daily Commitment consistency;
  • to create, confirm and reconcile commitment charges where enabled;
  • to prevent duplicate charges and maintain payment integrity;
  • to troubleshoot failures and maintain service security;
  • to respond to support enquiries.

5. Sharing

We may share the minimum information required with service providers that help host the backend, store application data, process payments or deliver infrastructure.

6. Retention

Records may be retained as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain payment and audit integrity, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and prevent duplicate or unauthorized transactions.

7. Security

RYZE uses measures intended to protect data, including server-side handling of payment credentials and keeping payment-provider secrets out of the mobile app.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to use paid Commitment modes. Contact RYZE regarding privacy requests using the address below.

9. Children

Paid commitment features are intended for users who are at least 18 years old or otherwise legally able to enter the applicable payment agreement.

10. Changes

RYZE is in private beta. This policy may change as the product develops.
